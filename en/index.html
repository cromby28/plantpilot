Welcome to PlantPilot Support

PlantPilot helps you care for your indoor and balcony plants based on actual need – with climate-adjusted watering intervals, optional Bluetooth thermo-hygrometers, on-device AI plant recognition, and shared household management via iCloud.

💧 Dynamic Care

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does PlantPilot Pro work (one-time purchase, no subscription)?

The free version of PlantPilot includes up to 5 plants, 1 Bluetooth sensor, and 3 free AI plant recognition trials. PlantPilot Pro is a single one-time in-app purchase (no subscription) that unlocks unlimited plants, unlimited Bluetooth sensors, unlimited AI recognition, automatic iCloud backup, and iCloud household sharing.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open the Climate tab in PlantPilot and tap “Restore Purchases” in the PlantPilot Pro section at the top. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If iCloud backup was active, your plant data will sync back automatically.

My Bluetooth thermo-hygrometer isn’t showing up – what can I do?

Check under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth that PlantPilot has Bluetooth access. Hold your iPhone close to the sensor during initial pairing and tap “Scan for Sensors Again” in the Climate tab.

How do household sharing and vacation mode work?

Only the household owner needs PlantPilot Pro (up to 5 members per household). Invited roommates or temporary vacation caretakers can join using the free version to view plants, log watering, and coordinate care.

Are my plant photos uploaded for AI recognition?

No. Plant recognition runs 100% locally on your iPhone using Apple Core ML and Vision. Your photos never leave your device unless you enable iCloud backup or share your household via iCloud.
