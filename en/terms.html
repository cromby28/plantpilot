2. Features (Free & PlantPilot Pro)

  • Free Version: Manage up to 5 own plants, connect 1 Bluetooth thermo-hygrometer, and use 3 free trials of on-device AI plant recognition.
  • PlantPilot Pro (One-Time Purchase, No Subscription): One-time in-app purchase via the Apple App Store unlocking unlimited plants, unlimited Bluetooth sensors, unlimited AI plant recognition, automatic iCloud backup, and household/vacation sharing.

3. Household Sharing & Member Roles

With PlantPilot Pro, a home can be shared via Apple iCloud (CloudKit) with up to 5 members in total. Invited guests without their own PlantPilot Pro license can view shared plants, log watering, and manage their own care logs; creating, editing, or deleting plants as well as changing climate or sensor settings is reserved for Pro owners/members. The owner may revoke shares and vacation passes at any time.
