2. Data Processing Inside the PlantPilot App

a) Local App Data

All content you create – in particular plant names, genera, room locations, notes, watering history, climate settings, and optional plant photos – is stored locally in the app sandbox on your iPhone. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the app contract).

In addition, the app stores technical state locally on your device (via UserDefaults and the iOS Keychain), such as onboarding completion and the number of free AI recognition trials used.

b) Camera, Photo Library, and On-Device AI Plant Recognition

When you take a photo of a plant or select one from your photo library, PlantPilot only accesses the images you explicitly choose (legal basis: your consent / device permission, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

AI-powered plant recognition runs 100% locally on your device using Apple Vision and Core ML. No photos are transmitted to our servers or third parties for analysis.

c) Bluetooth Low Energy (Thermo-Hygrometers)

If you enable sensor integration in the app settings and grant Bluetooth permission, PlantPilot scans for compatible nearby Bluetooth thermo-hygrometers to read temperature, relative humidity, signal strength, and device names.

This data is processed solely inside the app to calculate watering needs and is never used for location tracking or movement profiling (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / your Bluetooth permission).

d) Notifications (Local Reminders & Silent Sync Alerts)

With your explicit permission, PlantPilot schedules local iOS notifications to remind you when plants need watering. When iCloud household sharing is active, silent system notifications via Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) may be used to keep shared households in sync. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

e) Apple iCloud / CloudKit (Pro Backup & Household Sharing)

Users with PlantPilot Pro receive automatic data backup in their private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) as well as the ability to share their home with up to 5 people (roommates or temporary vacation caretakers) via iCloud sharing.

  • Storage and synchronization take place exclusively via the CloudKit infrastructure of Apple Inc. (or Apple Distribution International Ltd. in the EEA) under your Apple ID and Apple’s security and privacy controls.
  • As the app developer, we have no access to your private iCloud Apple ID or your private CloudKit databases.
  • When accepting a household invitation, your display name (if shared via iCloud) as well as shared plant records and care logs become visible to members of that shared household zone.

f) In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

Purchasing PlantPilot Pro (a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase) is processed entirely through the Apple App Store (StoreKit 2). Payment details are handled exclusively by Apple; we only receive an anonymous entitlement verification from iOS confirming that Pro features are unlocked.

g) No Ads, No Tracking, No Third-Party Analytics

PlantPilot does not integrate any advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics SDKs.

4. Email Support Inquiries

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the information you provide solely to respond to your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete the correspondence once resolved unless statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Storage Duration & Deletion

You can delete local app data at any time inside the app or remove all local data by uninstalling PlantPilot. Data backed up to your private iCloud account can also be managed or deleted in iOS under [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

  • Request erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR),

  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR),

  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
